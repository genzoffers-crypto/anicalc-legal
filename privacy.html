About this policy

AniCalc is a calculator and study toolkit developed by an individual developer in Nepal. Contact AniCalc at anicalcsupport@gmail.com for support, privacy questions or requests about information you have sent us.

Information on your device

Calculation history, preferences, themes, favourites, saved AI conversations, usage allowances and reward expiry dates are stored locally. API keys use the device secure storage integration. AniCalc does not operate a backend that receives your calculations, conversations or keys. Device storage is not a guarantee against access on a compromised device or through system backups.

Age choice and AI access

We ask your age to determine access to AI features. Only your age group (under 16, 16–17, or 18 and over) is retained on your device, not your exact age or birth date. Provider access depends on your age and the provider’s requirements. Some providers require users to be 18 or older. DeepSeek setup for ages 16–17 asks a parent or legal guardian to review the provider policies and approve use and possible API charges. That approval is stored on this device and removed when the DeepSeek key is deleted. It is a declaration, not verified parental identity. AniCalc restricts all other AI providers to users aged 18 and over. This self-declaration is not identity verification and does not replace the terms of an AI provider.

Optional AI services

When you use AI Solve or Error Detective, your request, relevant conversation context and attached content are sent directly to the provider you select. Your API key authenticates that request. Custom endpoints receive the information required for the request. If you enable backup models, a failed request may be sent to another selected provider. Those services have their own privacy policies, retention periods, charges and international processing arrangements. Deleting content in AniCalc does not delete copies retained by an external provider. Do not send sensitive information you do not want that provider to receive.

Permissions and media

Camera and photo access let you attach images and personalize the app. Microphone access supports voice input. Speech recognition and speech playback use device services; depending on your installed service and settings, processing may involve its servers. Exported formula images are saved to your gallery. You can manage these permissions in Android settings.

Online content

Theme, formula and sound downloads contact GitHub and jsDelivr. Runtime font requests may contact Google Fonts. These services receive normal connection information, including your IP address. Their own privacy practices apply.

Advertising and diagnostics

The current version integrates Google Mobile Ads using Google test ad identifiers. The SDK can process device and network information, advertising interactions and diagnostic information. Rewarded ads are optional. The current configuration requests child-restricted treatment and general-audience ad content. AniCalc does not use a separate app analytics or crash-reporting service in this version. This does not exclude diagnostic processing by Android, Google Play, advertising SDKs or other services you use.

Retention and your choices

Local information remains until removed through the app, Android app storage controls or uninstallation, subject to device backup behaviour. Removing a saved API key removes the app’s stored copy; revoke it with its provider if needed. Gallery exports and shared files remain outside the app until you delete them. Information you email us is used to answer your request and retained only as needed for that purpose or legal obligations. Contact us to request access, correction or deletion of information you have sent us. Rights and available complaint procedures depend on your location.

Children

Calculator and study tools can be used without enabling AI. We do not ask children to create an AniCalc account or send us personal details. Parents can contact us about information a child has sent us. Age controls do not replace parental supervision or external-service requirements.

This website

These static pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. They contain no advertising, analytics scripts or contact forms. GitHub processes connection information to provide and secure hosting under its privacy statement.
